AwazPe – Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your AwazPe account from within the app by opening Settings and selecting Delete Account. You will need to verify your registered mobile number using an OTP.

Request Account Deletion

If you cannot access the app, you can contact AwazPe Support to request account deletion. Please contact us using your registered mobile number so we can verify your request.

Support phone: 9926821853

Contact Support on WhatsApp

What Happens After Your Request?

We will verify your identity before processing the request. Once deletion is completed, your AwazPe Firebase Authentication account will be deleted, and locally stored payment data will be cleared when you use the in-app deletion process.

Locally stored data on a device that you cannot access may need to be removed separately from that device.

Account deletion is permanent. You may need to register again to use AwazPe in the future.

Read our Privacy Policy.
