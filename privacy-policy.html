AlertMe – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2 October 2026

AlertMe ("we", "us", or "our") is an Android application designed to identify payment notifications and provide voice alerts for received payment amounts.

1. Information We Use

Mobile Number:
AlertMe uses your mobile number for account authentication through Firebase Phone Authentication.

Notification Information:
When you enable Android Notification Access for AlertMe, the app processes notification information such as the notification title, text, and expanded text to identify payment-related notifications.

Payment Information:
When a received payment notification is detected, AlertMe stores the detected payment amount and timestamp locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

The information processed by AlertMe is used to:

  • Identify received payment notifications.
  • Detect the payment amount.
  • Provide a voice alert for the detected amount.
  • Display the most recently detected payment.
  • Maintain payment history locally on the device.
  • Authenticate users through mobile OTP.

3. Notification Access

AlertMe requires Android Notification Access to identify payment notifications. This permission is controlled by you through the Android system settings.

Notification information is processed by the application for payment detection. The current AlertMe implementation does not upload payment notification content or payment history to an AlertMe server.

4. Local Storage

Detected payment amounts and timestamps are stored locally on your Android device to provide the last payment and payment history features.

The current implementation does not automatically expire or delete this locally stored payment history. Local application data can be removed using Android's available app-data management controls.

5. Firebase Authentication

AlertMe uses Firebase Authentication for mobile phone-number authentication.

Firebase may process information required to provide authentication and security services, including your phone number and certain technical information such as IP address, user-agent information, Firebase Android App ID, and security/verification information where applicable.

Google may use and store phone numbers provided for Firebase Phone Authentication for spam and abuse prevention across its services.

Firebase and Google's own privacy practices may apply to information processed through their services.

6. Voice Alerts

AlertMe uses the Android Text-to-Speech functionality to speak the detected payment amount. The voice alert is generated on the device.

7. Data Sharing

The current AlertMe implementation does not upload payment notification content or locally stored payment history to an AlertMe server.

However, third-party services such as Firebase may process information necessary to provide authentication, security, and related services.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Controls

  • You can enable or disable Notification Access through Android system settings.
  • You can manage or remove locally stored application data using Android's app-data controls.
  • You can choose whether to use AlertMe's notification and voice alert functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

AlertMe is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly target children with the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its functionality, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will include a revised "Last Updated" date.
